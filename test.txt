  Transport Operations (Marine Safety) (Riverston Bay Marine Zone) Amendment Regulation 2024
  1 Short title
  This regulation may be cited as the Transport Operations (Marine Safety) (Riverston Bay Marine Zone) Amendment Regulation 2024.
  2 Commencement
  This regulation commences on 1 July 2024.
  3 Regulation amended
  This regulation amends the Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016.
  4 Amendment of sch 5 (Marine zones)
  Schedule 5—
  insert—
  Part 3 Riverston Bay
  1 Marine zone
  (1) The area of Riverston Bay is a marine zone.
  (2) The area of Riverston Bay is the area within the following boundary—
  • starting at latitude 24º 04.363' south, longitude 151º 17.988' east on the western shore of the mouth of the bay
  • then generally south-easterly to latitude 24º 04.457' south, longitude 151º 18.127' east on the eastern shore of the mouth of the bay
  • then along the shore of the bay to the starting point.

  2 Requirement for marine zone
  The requirement for the marine zone is that a ship powered by an engine must not be operated in the marine zone.
  3 Exceptions from requirement for marine zone
  The requirement in section 2 does not apply to the operation of a ship by an employee of the Gladstone Area Water Board who is—
  (a) a shipping inspector performing a function under section 153 of the Act; or
  (b) carrying out a water activity under the Water Act 2000 relating to the board’s main function as a water authority under section 569 of that Act; or
  (c) carrying out an activity under an authority issued to the board under the Fisheries Act 1994, section 49 for the following purposes—
  (i) broodstock collection;
  (ii) education;
  (iii) fish stocking.
